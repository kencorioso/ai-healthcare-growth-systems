# HEOS Evidence Engine — Video 3: Different Healthcare Vertical

**Status:** FINAL — PASS / FROZEN (Workstream G calibration revision)
**Tagline:** With the Organization. Not the Technology.
**Persona:** Hiring manager / executive in another healthcare vertical
**Core question:** Can Ken enter a healthcare environment he doesn't already know, understand how the organization and its evidence actually work, and build an appropriate analytical approach around that reality without pretending he already knows the answer?
**Target length:** approximately 80–88 seconds

## Full Script

If I walked into an orthopedic organization tomorrow, AI wouldn't be the first thing on my mind. I'd start by learning how that organization actually works.

I'd want to understand how a patient moves from referral and authorization, through evaluation and treatment, and, when surgery is involved, into rehabilitation and follow-up. I'd want to know which systems capture each step, where handoffs occur, and where the evidence stops connecting.

Only then would I start deciding what to build.

That's the same discipline behind Harbor Ridge. I designed its operating environment and the relationships in its data first. Then I built the synthetic testbed and evaluation around that.

I wouldn't take Harbor Ridge's schema and drop it into another healthcare organization. A different organization needs a different system built around it.

What transfers is the process — understand the reality first, build the evidence architecture around it, then test whether the resulting analysis actually deserves to be trusted.

That's what Harbor Ridge demonstrates — not a system that works everywhere, but a process I've already run once, end to end. Put me in front of a different organization, and I'd start the same way. With the organization. Not the technology.

## The One Sentence the Viewer Should Remember

"Put me in front of a different organization, and I'd start the same way. With the organization. Not the technology."

## Guardrails

- This is a hiring pitch about demonstrated building capability, not a claim that the Harbor Ridge implementation itself is portable.
- Harbor Ridge is explicitly synthetic and designed, not a real organization observed or entered. The script states "I designed its operating environment" specifically to prevent any inference that Harbor Ridge was a real organization Ken mapped by observation.
- Portability remains scoped to the method and process, not the Harbor Ridge schema, product implementation, or demonstrated cross-industry performance.
- The orthopedic patient-journey description (referral, authorization, evaluation, treatment, rehabilitation, follow-up) is illustrative of what Ken would investigate in an unfamiliar organization — verified against real orthopedic industry patterns (referral/authorization delays, post-surgical follow-through gaps) — and is not a claim of prior orthopedic operating experience or clinical expertise.
- The logistics analogy from the original Workstream E version has been removed. Orthopedics alone carries the illustrative portability example.
- Trust is something the resulting analysis must earn through evaluation, not something a well-designed evidence architecture produces automatically. This preserves consistency with the frozen V0.2 result: Scenario 1 MISS, Scenario 2 MISS, Healthy Baseline CLEAN.
- Nothing here claims autonomous diagnostic capability, production deployment, or general healthcare validation.
- This version reframes the video around demonstrated investigative sequence (organization and evidence understood before any technology decision) rather than a defensive portability disclaimer. Supersedes the original Workstream E version.
