# Harbor Ridge Behavioral Health

## Version 1 — Domain Validation Scenario

> **Project Status: Active Development**

Harbor Ridge Behavioral Health is the fictional healthcare organization that will serve as the Version 1 validation environment for the AI Healthcare Growth Systems Patient Acquisition & Executive Insight Engine.

Harbor Ridge will represent a behavioral healthcare organization providing substance use disorder and related behavioral health treatment services.

## Why Behavioral Health for Version 1

Version 1 is intentionally being developed within a healthcare environment where the project owner has direct operating and marketing experience.

The purpose is not to limit the framework to behavioral healthcare.

The purpose is to establish a domain-grounded environment in which assumptions about patient acquisition, qualification, admissions, marketing attribution, operational constraints, and executive decision-making can be challenged against real-world experience.
