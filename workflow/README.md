# Workflow

This directory documents the workflows that connect data, analytics, AI, and executive reporting within the AI Healthcare Growth Systems project.

The goal is to build repeatable processes that transform healthcare marketing data into useful decision support.

## Planned Workflow

The initial system will follow this general sequence:

1. Ingest synthetic healthcare marketing data
2. Validate and organize the data
3. Calculate marketing and acquisition performance metrics
4. Identify meaningful changes, anomalies, or performance shifts
5. Pass relevant analytical findings to the AI layer
6. Generate observations, hypotheses, and recommended areas for investigation
7. Present findings through an executive dashboard or summary
8. Preserve human review before decisions or actions are taken

## Design Principle

**Automate the movement of information, not executive judgment.**

Automation should reduce repetitive analytical and reporting work while keeping important marketing and business decisions under human review.

## Planned Tools

The workflow may incorporate:

- Python
- SQL
- AI/LLM tools
- n8n or similar workflow automation
- Dashboard and reporting tools

The final architecture will evolve as individual components are built and tested.
